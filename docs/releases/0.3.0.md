# CrossDesk Reader 0.3.0

CrossDesk Reader 0.3.0 adds the Coverflow Home theme and overhauls Todo on the
BOOX Picco. These changes are measured from the `crossdesk-v0.2.0` release.

## Todo

- **Priorities and sorting.** Tap an open task's priority badge to cycle through
  no priority, `!`, `!!` and `!!!`. Sort open tasks by priority, due date,
  creation date or added order. Completed tasks stay below in saved order.
  Priority and date edits keep rows in place while the list is open. Reopen
  Todo from Home or select a sort again to apply the new order.
- **Task editor.** Tap a task to edit its text and due date on one screen.
  New tasks open the selected full keyboard or T9 keypad first. Save writes
  the text and date together. Back discards the draft. To delete one task
  from the editor, clear its text, save and confirm.
- **Dates and overdue status.** A trusted local clock records the creation
  date when you add a task. In the editor, move the due date by a day or a
  week, use Up or Down to move by a day, or choose Today, Tomorrow or No date.
  The list and editor show when a task is due or overdue. Saved calendar dates
  stay visible when the clock is untrusted. The 32-task limit still applies.
- **List layout.** The New task button stays above the list. Larger checkboxes,
  a pencil beside editable descriptions, compact priority badges and readable
  date lines make the rows easier to scan. Scroll through up to six visible
  task rows. Completed tasks keep their priority and hide the priority button.
- **Home previews.** Desk and Coverflow show the first three open tasks in the
  chosen order, with priority marks and due dates. The sort screen explains
  this shared order. An asterisk marks an overdue task when there is room and
  the clock is trusted. Tap the preview to open Todo.
- **Saved tasks.** Existing tasks load with their text and completion state
  intact. They start without priority or dates. If you return to 0.2.0, its
  next Todo save drops the new priority, date and sort fields. Back up both
  `/.crosspoint/todo-a.json` and `/.crosspoint/todo-b.json` before downgrading.

## Coverflow Home

- Choose **Settings → Display → UI Theme → Coverflow** on a device with
  touch and PSRAM, such as the Picco. It shows up to ten recent books in a
  portrait carousel. Swipe left or right, press Up or Down, or tap a side
  cover to bring another book to the centre. Tap the centre cover to open it.
  Back leaves Home in place.
- Swipe up or tap the bottom arrow to open the apps sheet. It includes
  Browse Files, Library, Settings, Pomodoro and Sudoku, plus the Plugins or
  OPDS shortcut when available. Tap above the sheet, swipe down or press
  Back to close it.
- The **Reading** card shows this month's minutes, your streak and the last
  seven days against your goal. Tap it to open monthly Statistics. The
  **Todo** card opens tasks, and the **Weather** card previews the saved
  forecast and opens Weather.
- The optional progress line is on by default. It shows the centre book's
  title, saved progress for supported formats, and recorded reading time for
  EPUBs. The title, bar and values follow the visible cover width. Narrow
  covers use compact labels and show the values that fit. Turn the line off
  under **Settings → Display → Coverflow Progress** to centre the cover.
- The centre cover has a dithered halo and a larger thumbnail than its
  neighbours. Books without artwork use a title card. Empty Home offers
  an **Open Library** button.
- Coverflow caches centre covers in PSRAM for revisits, reuses its thumbnails
  when you toggle progress, and remembers when a book has no cover to avoid
  repeated generation attempts. Periodic half refreshes help reduce e-ink
  ghosting while browsing.

## Smaller changes

- Viewing saved TXT or Markdown progress in Coverflow leaves stale conversion
  caches intact for the reader to handle.
- The FreeInk SDK update gives Todo larger checkbox targets and independent
  row controls, and keeps the edit icon clear of wrapped task text.
- Release firmware files now use `crossdesk-<version>-<board>.bin`. The
  release workflow and update lookup use the same naming. On boards with
  in-app updates, a 0.2.0 build that looks for the old `crosspoint-…` asset
  name needs a manual update to this release.

## Installing on the Picco

Install `crossdesk-0.3.0-picco.bin` over USB. Back up the full flash before
your first firmware upload. Write the app at `0xe0000`. The stock bootloader
and recovery partition stay in place.

```sh
esptool.py --chip esp32s3 write_flash 0xe0000 crossdesk-0.3.0-picco.bin
```

If you build from source, `pio run -e picco -t upload` also writes only the
app slot. Keep your existing SD card data when upgrading.

CrossDesk Reader is an unofficial fork of CrossPoint Reader for the BOOX
Picco. The combined firmware uses GPLv3 because it includes wolfSSL.
